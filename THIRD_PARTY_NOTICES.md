## External services and data

- **Perfect Corp. YouCam API** creates the optional apparel virtual try-on.
  Use is subject to the applicable YouCam API terms and privacy policy.
- **OpenAI API** interprets clothing requests into constrained structured
  requirements. Use is subject to the applicable OpenAI terms and data
  controls.
- **Cloudflare Pages, Workers and Turnstile** host and protect the public
  application.
- **OpenStreetMap contributors** provide community map data used by the
  alteration-service finder. Map data is credited in the interface and is
  subject to the Open Database Licence where applicable. Nominatim and
  Overpass provide search endpoints for the low-traffic prototype.

Names and marks are used only to identify the services integrated into or
used by the project. No third-party logos are bundled into the interface.
