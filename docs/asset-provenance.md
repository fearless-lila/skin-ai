## Catalogue and product data

All eight product listings in `data/mock-catalogue.json` are fictional mock
products created for AccessWear. This includes product names, the fictional
retailer name `AccessWear Demo Catalogue`, prices, sizes, garment measurements,
accessibility attributes and product URLs.

The interface labels the listings as demo products. No product is presented
as a real item available for sale, and no retailer product data is scraped or
copied.

## Generated images

The following image groups were generated specifically for AccessWear using
ChatGPT/Codex at the direction of the entrant:

- Product and YouCam reference images under `frontend/images/`, including the
  Avery, Casey, Ellis, Maya, Nora, Rowan, Samira and Taylor garments.
- The AccessWear hero and alteration-support hero illustrations.
- The four accessible alteration-support illustrations.
- The chest and waist measurement illustration supplied as `measurement.png`.

The prompts and iteration decisions were directed by the entrant for this
project. The images are not real retailer photographs, business photographs,
or photographs of identifiable members of the public. No third-party logos or
brand marks are intentionally included.

## Runtime user and provider images

User-uploaded photographs are not repository assets. A user must confirm that
they are the person shown or have permission to use the photograph before it
is sent to YouCam.

Virtual try-on results are generated at runtime by the Perfect Corp. YouCam
API from the user's authorised photograph and a generated garment reference.
They are not included as static submission assets unless the entrant has the
necessary permission from the person shown.

## Submission-video rule

The demonstration video should use a photograph of the entrant or another
adult who has expressly agreed to the recording and YouCam processing. It
should not contain unlicensed music, retailer logos, real business marks or
other third-party copyrighted material.
